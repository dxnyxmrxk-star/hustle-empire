URBAN TYCOON V19.2 — CITY BUSINESS CARD LAYOUT STATIC AUDIT

Root cause found:
- Original City business card grid reserved only 54px for the building image.
- A later global sprite rule forced .district-business-image to
  --sprite-business = 72px on desktop (66px/60px at mobile breakpoints).
- The image therefore became physically wider than its grid column and invaded
  the text region, where income / "Доход / с" were rendered.
- The old income label also used a negative top margin, making the visual
  collision worse.

V19.2 changes:
- Card markup split into four explicit regions:
  1. district-business-media
  2. district-business-copy
  3. district-business-stats
  4. district-business-actions
- Card uses a strict two-column grid: building media + minmax(0,1fr) content.
- City owns its own --city-business-art-size and overrides legacy
  --sprite-business dimensions.
- Image/sprite/canvas is capped to 100% of its media column.
- overflow:hidden on media frame prevents any painting into the text region.
- Name can use up to two lines.
- Status chip is static-flow, not positioned.
- Income label and numeric value live in a dedicated stats box.
- Removed negative-margin behavior from the income label through final override.
- Button owns its own action row and no longer depends on margin-top:auto.
- <=520px uses one business card per row while preserving media/text columns.
- <=360px reduces art size/padding without collapsing the text column.
